# Kildare Clinic privacy policy

Source: https://www.kildaredoc.ie/privacy

How Kildare Clinic uses the information patients give through its website: appointment bookings, online medical certificate requests, payments taken by Stripe or PayPal, the contact form and optional cookies. It lists who the clinic shares data with, explains data protection rights and how to complain to the Data Protection Commission.
