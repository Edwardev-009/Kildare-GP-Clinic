# Medical certificates at Kildare Clinic

Source: https://www.kildaredoc.ie/medical-certificate

Kildare Clinic offers online medical certificates issued by Irish-registered GPs. You request and pay online, a doctor phones you, and an approved certificate is sent by email or WhatsApp within 1–2 hours. No clinic visit is needed. Certificates are issued only where clinically appropriate, and you are refunded if one cannot be issued.

## Certificates and prices

- Sick Certificate: €25. An official note from a GP confirming you're unwell and unfit for work or study, for short-term minor illness.
- Unfit for Travel Certificate: €25. A GP certificate stating you are medically unfit to travel, commonly needed for airline, hotel or travel insurance claims.
- Fit to Travel Certificate: €25. A GP certificate confirming you are medically fit to travel, for airlines, tour operators or insurers that ask for one.
- Return-to-Work / Fit for Work Certificate: €25. A GP certificate confirming you have recovered and are fit to return to work or study.

## How it works

1. Request and pay online: Choose your certificate, fill in a short form and pay securely by card or PayPal. There is no need to visit the clinic.
2. A doctor calls you: One of our Irish-registered GPs phones you to ask a few questions about the reason for your certificate.
3. Receive your certificate: If a certificate is appropriate, it is sent to you by email or WhatsApp within 1–2 hours.

## Not included

- Backdated (retrospective) certificates
- Certificates the doctor decides are not clinically appropriate (you are refunded)
- Long-term illness or detailed medical reports

## Questions

**Can I get a medical certificate online in Ireland?** Yes. Request your certificate on this page, pay online and a Kildare Clinic GP will phone you. If a certificate is appropriate, it is sent to you within 1–2 hours by email or WhatsApp. You do not need to visit the clinic.

**Do I need to visit the clinic?** No. The whole process is online and by phone. The doctor speaks to you by phone instead of seeing you in person.

**How much does an online medical certificate cost?** Every medical certificate costs €25, paid online by card or PayPal when you make the request.

**How long does it take to get my certificate?** The doctor calls you after your payment, and an approved certificate is sent within 1–2 hours, during clinic opening hours.

**Will I definitely get a certificate?** Certificates are only issued where the doctor considers it clinically appropriate. If a certificate can't be issued, the doctor will explain why and you will be refunded in full.

**Can I get a backdated certificate?** No. We can't issue backdated certificates.

**Is my payment secure?** Yes. Payment is taken on the secure pages of Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or PayPal. Kildare Clinic never sees or stores your card details.
