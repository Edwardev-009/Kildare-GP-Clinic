# Medical certificates at Kildare Clinic

Source: https://www.kildaredoc.ie/medical-certificate

Kildare Clinic offers medical certificates requested online and reviewed by Irish-registered GPs. Certificates are issued within 5 hours and only where clinically appropriate.

## Certificates and prices

- Sick Certificate: €30. An official note from a GP confirming you're unwell and unfit for work or study, for short-term minor illness.
- Unfit for Travel Certificate: €40. A GP certificate stating you are medically unfit to travel, commonly needed for airline, hotel or travel insurance claims.
- Fit to Travel Certificate: €40. A GP certificate confirming you are medically fit to travel, for airlines, tour operators or insurers that ask for one.
- Return-to-Work / Fit for Work Certificate: €30. A GP certificate confirming you have recovered and are fit to return to work or study.

## How it works

1. Send your request: Choose your certificate and fill in a short form with your details and what you need it for.
2. Pay and speak to the doctor: We email you a secure payment link, and one of our GPs calls you to verify your request.
3. Receive your certificate: Once approved, your certificate is issued and sent to you securely by email.

## Not included

- Backdated (retrospective) certificates
- Certificates that need a physical examination
- Long-term illness or detailed medical reports

## Questions

**How quickly will I receive my medical certificate?** Certificates are issued within 5 hours once the doctor has verified your request. You'll first receive a payment link by email, and a GP will call you to verify your details.

**How much does a medical certificate cost?** Sick and Return-to-Work certificates cost €30. Unfit for Travel and Fit to Travel certificates cost €40.

**Will I definitely get a certificate?** Certificates are only issued where the doctor considers it clinically appropriate. If a certificate can't be issued, the doctor will explain why and, where needed, advise you to be seen in person.

**Who reviews my request?** Every request is reviewed by an Irish-registered GP at Kildare Clinic, who will call you to verify your request before a certificate is issued.

**Can I get a backdated certificate?** No. We can't issue backdated certificates, or certificates that require a physical examination. For those, please visit the clinic during opening hours.
