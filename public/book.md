# Book an appointment online at Kildare Clinic

Source: https://www.kildaredoc.ie/book

Patients can book a GP appointment or a blood test online. Blood tests are by online appointment only and are offered on Fridays. Choose a service, then a morning or evening session, pick a free time and pay online (card or PayPal) or at the clinic.
