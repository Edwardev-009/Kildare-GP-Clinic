# Kildare Clinic cookies and browser storage

Source: https://www.kildaredoc.ie/cookies

Google Analytics and Google Maps embeds are optional. Neither loads until its separate category is allowed. Visitors can reject both, accept both or save separate preferences; Cookie settings in the footer reopens the choices. Contact details and website pages remain available when both are rejected.

## Storage

- Kildare Clinic local storage, kildare_cookie_preferences: remembers the saved analytics/maps choices and date. The choice is valid for 180 days; the browser entry remains until cleared, updated or removed by the website.
- Google Analytics cookies, _ga and _ga_*: distinguish visits and measure website use only when analytics is allowed. This website configures an expiry of up to 180 days from cookie creation and disables automatic expiry updates.
- Google Maps embed: may use Google cookies or other browser storage only after maps is allowed. Provider cookie names and durations depend on Google, account and browser settings; no complete fixed inventory is asserted.

Allowing analytics sends usage and browser/device information to Google. Loading maps sends IP address and browser information to Google. Google Fonts requests still load when optional services are rejected. Ordinary external Google Maps/WhatsApp links open those services only when followed.

## Withdrawal

Save a category as off in Cookie settings to withdraw it. Maps are removed. If analytics has started loading, site-accessible Google Analytics cookies are cleared and the page reloads to stop the tag; unsent form entries may be lost. The site cannot clear Google-domain cookies or retract data already sent.
